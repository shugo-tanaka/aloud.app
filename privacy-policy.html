Privacy Policy

Aloud — Last updated: September 2026

Aloud is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data on any external server. Everything stays on your device or your personal iCloud account.

Data We Collect and How It's Used

Goals and Block Groups
The goals and block group configurations you create are stored locally on your device and synced to your personal iCloud account via Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage. This data never leaves your Apple ecosystem and is not accessible to us.

Speech Recognition
Aloud uses on-device speech recognition to verify your goal recitations. Your voice data is processed entirely on your device using Apple's SFSpeechRecognizer framework. No audio or transcripts are sent to any server.

Usage Analytics
Aloud logs anonymized usage events (such as recitation attempts and outcomes) locally on your device to power features like your focus streak and focus rate. This data never leaves your device.

Screen Time Data
Aloud uses Apple's Screen Time APIs (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity) to monitor and block app usage. In accordance with Apple's Family Controls terms, this data is used solely to provide the app blocking and recitation features on your own device. It is never used for advertising, shared with third parties, or transmitted off your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any account information
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties
  • We do not have access to which apps you choose to block

Children's Privacy

Aloud is intended for users who are managing their own device usage for personal focus and productivity purposes. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the date at the top of this policy.
